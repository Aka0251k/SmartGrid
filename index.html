Help & Safety

Simple explanations and practical guidance for everyday users.

ϟ

Understand your numbers

kW is the rate electricity is being used at a moment. kWh is the amount of energy used over time. Higher usage isn't automatically unsafe; context and equipment ratings matter.

♧

Energy-saving habits

  • Switch off lights and appliances when not needed.
  • Unplug chargers and devices when appropriate.
  • Use efficient lighting and maintain appliances.
  • Coordinate high-power appliance use only under building guidance.
!

If an overload warning appears

If safe, reduce nonessential loads and follow your building's safety procedure. If you notice heat, smoke, sparks, burning smells, or other danger, keep away and contact qualified emergency support. Never open electrical panels or handle exposed wiring.

☎

Official support contacts

Official contact numbers depend on the installation location and responsible electricity provider. An administrator should verify and add the correct contacts before this section is used in a live deployment.
