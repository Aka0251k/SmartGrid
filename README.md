# SmartGrid
An IoT-based energy monitoring platform that helps communities track electricity usage, detect potential overloads, receive alerts, and build smarter energy-saving habits.
# ⚡ SmartGrid

### Smarter Energy. Brighter Communities.

SmartGrid is an IoT-based energy monitoring and management platform designed to help student hostels, shared residential buildings, estates, and communities better understand and manage their electricity usage.

The goal is simple: **make energy usage easier to understand, potential overloads easier to identify, and smarter energy decisions easier for everyone.**

## 💡 The Problem

In shared electricity environments, people often have little visibility into how electricity is being consumed. This can contribute to unexpected outages, potential overloads, inefficient energy use, and disagreements about electricity distribution.

Without accessible usage information, it is difficult for residents and administrators to understand what is happening or make informed decisions.

## 🚀 The Vision

SmartGrid aims to bring electricity monitoring, useful insights, and practical guidance into one accessible platform.

### Planned Features

- **📊 Energy Dashboard:** View electricity usage, consumption trends, and system status.
- **⚡ Overload Alerts:** Identify potential overload conditions and notify users.
- **📡 IoT Integration:** Connect compatible monitoring devices to send electricity readings to the platform.
- **🤖 AI Energy Assistant:** Explain usage data, recommend energy-saving habits, and guide users through basic troubleshooting.
- **📈 Usage Analytics:** Understand consumption patterns and support more informed energy decisions.
- **📶 Low-Connectivity Support:** Make essential information accessible when internet connectivity is limited.
- **🛟 Help & Safety:** Provide understandable safety guidance and verified contact information for relevant electricity authorities.

## 🎯 Who Is SmartGrid For?

- Student hostels and university communities
- Shared residential buildings and estates
- Community energy projects and mini-grid operators
- Building administrators responsible for monitoring electricity usage

## 🛠️ Development Approach

SmartGrid is being developed in stages.

The initial focus is a responsive, easy-to-use web interface with simulated energy readings. Future development will introduce persistent data storage, user authentication, IoT device connectivity, AI-powered explanations, and low-connectivity features.

Simulated readings will be clearly labelled and will not be presented as real sensor measurements.

## 🌍 Potential Impact

SmartGrid aims to help communities:

- Understand how electricity is being used.
- Recognize unusual usage and potential overloads.
- Develop more efficient energy habits.
- Improve visibility into shared electricity consumption.
- Make better-informed decisions about energy management.

SmartGrid is intended as a monitoring and information platform, not a replacement for certified electrical protection systems or qualified electrical professionals.
